The Entry Ladder

A clear path into the room.

We do not expect polished finality on day one. Progression is stepped.

1. The 7-Day Challenge

A short sprint to test your focus, show your raw thinking, and clear the initial bar.

2. Live Failure Lab

An entry point session where rough concepts and imperfect work are picked apart constructively.

3. 10-Week Cohort

An intensive deep-dive team studio built entirely around real briefs and structured standards.

African creatives do not have a talent problem. They have a system problem.

Community is still the oldest operating system. The Let's Create Network builds the structure that unlocks it for creative work. Everything inside the network exists to produce stronger creatives through structure, not scattered encouragement.

  • Talented people are learning entirely alone.
  • Feedback is highly inconsistent or shallow.
  • Good work rarely compounds into real, permanent opportunity.
What we build

Three pillars. One system.

Everything inside The Let's Create Network is built around one idea: better systems produce better creatives.

01

Cohort Studio

Short, serious cohort cycles where mixed-skill teams learn to work like a full-stack creative unit. You leave with stronger process and one serious body of work.

  • Creative direction, design, copy, strategy, and production.
  • Real briefs, deadlines, and critique culture.
  • Outputs can include campaigns, brands, content, video, or digital products.
02

Community Network

A living system where participants stay connected long after a single cohort ends. You keep the room even after the cycle ends.

  • Peer review, learning loops, and shared standards.
  • Alumni pathways into future cohorts and leadership.
  • Long-term belonging, not one-off participation.
03

Opportunity Engine

Proof, mentorship, and partnerships that move strong creatives closer to real opportunity. Access to reviews, briefs, visibility, and selected work opportunities as the network grows.

  • Access to partner networks, reviews, and design sprints.
  • Merit-based progression inside the network.
  • Opportunity routed through trust and quality.
How learning works

Build real work, not practice work.

The cohort is built around real work. Teams move through strategy, concept, design, copy, critique, and refinement until the final output holds together.

  • Weeks 1–2: Orientation and team formation.
  • Weeks 3–5: Strategy and core ideation.
  • Weeks 6–8: Hard execution and continuous critique.
  • Weeks 9–10: Final refinement and public network showcase.
Why the system compounds

Grow in public. Stay in the system.

After the cohort, creatives don't disappear. Their work, relationships, and growth stay inside the system and keep compounding. Alumni become the mentors for the next intake.

  • Cross-pollination is built into the workflow.
  • An organic progress route verified via the system.
  • Opportunities flow through the network because the work is trusted.
